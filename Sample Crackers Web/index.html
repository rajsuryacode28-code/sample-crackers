About us

A Family Business, Built for Festive Moments

Diya Burst Crackers is a family-run store based in Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu. For over 15 years, we've helped families celebrate Diwali with a carefully curated range of crackers, sparklers and gift combos — all sourced from licensed manufacturers and checked for safety before they reach your hands.

We believe Diwali is about togetherness, light and joy. That's why we keep our pricing transparent, our advice honest, and our stock fresh every season.
